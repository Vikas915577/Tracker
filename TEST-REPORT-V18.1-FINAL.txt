## Important fix found during testing
The first V18.1 package had a runtime scoping problem: a sibling IIFE tried to access V18-private `v18Render` and sprint state. That could cause a blank page at startup even though `node --check` passed. The package was corrected to integrate through the outer `render()` function and to use V18.1-owned menu/sprint state.
